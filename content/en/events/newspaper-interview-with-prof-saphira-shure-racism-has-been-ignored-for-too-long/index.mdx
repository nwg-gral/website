Structural racism has existed in Germany for a long time, but it has simply not been talked about, says Saphira Shure, professor of education at Bielefeld University. Parties such as the AfD have benefited from this, among others. 

A newspaper interview with GraL Prof. Saphira Shure, on July 12, 2023, in the “Neue Westfälische” newspaper.

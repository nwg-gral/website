Sensitivity to racism – education in a society of migration: Professor Saphira Shure in an interview with Christiane Attig and Rebecca Moltmann about her path to research and teaching, dealing with racism in schools, her dissertation, GraL, and racism research under racist structures. 

In the science podcast “Science S\*heroes,” Christiane Attig and Rebecca Moltmann talk to women and non-binary people about their research topics and their path into science. They jump across disciplines, but always address the specific perspective of marginalized groups in (and on) the science system.

Science S\*heroes is part of wissenschaftspodcasts.de.

The GraL project says farewell to its project member Roya Saadati Fashtomi as of September 15, 2026. Roya has been with the GraL project since its start and was responsible for the document analysis sub-study. With the completion of this sub-study, she is now moving on to a career in school teaching. We would like to thank her for her time with us and her outstanding work, and we wish her all the best in her future endeavors!

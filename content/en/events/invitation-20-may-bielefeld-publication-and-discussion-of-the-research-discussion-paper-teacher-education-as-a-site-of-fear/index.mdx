**Invitation to the Publication and Discussion**

The first results of our study have been published: in the form of a discussion paper titled “[**Teacher Education as a Site of Fear? Racism Professionalized**](https://pub.uni-bielefeld.de/download/3014016/3015928/Schitow_etal.2026_Impulspapier.pdf),” we offer insights into key findings from our ongoing research. We would like to discuss these results with you and warmly invite you to the presentation followed by a discussion.

**Wednesday, 20 May 2026 | 4:00–6:00 p.m. in Bielefeld in the lecture hall at the Oberstufen-Kolleg.** The event will take place in a hybrid format and can be attended either in person in Bielefeld or online via Zoom.

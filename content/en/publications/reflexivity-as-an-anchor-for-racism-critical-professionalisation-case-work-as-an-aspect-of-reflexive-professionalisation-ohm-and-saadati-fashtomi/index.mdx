This article presents the workshop Reflexivity as an anchor for anti-racist professionalisation? It highlights the relevance for teachers of engaging with anti-racism and outlines the multifaceted phenomenon of “racism.” This is followed by an examination of the terms “racism,” “critical racism perspectices", and “racism in and through school.” Building on this, case work is presented as a concrete method for practicing and approaching anti-racist professionalism in the mode of reflexive engagement. /Critical Race Theory is seen as a cross-cutting task in teacher education.&#x20;

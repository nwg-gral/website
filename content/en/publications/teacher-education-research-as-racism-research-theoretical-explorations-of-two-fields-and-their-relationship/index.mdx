This article examines the potential and synergy taht can be achieved by linking teacher education research with research into educational racism research. To this end, German-language teacher education research is systematically outlined as a diverse field and then briefly introduced in research on teacher education and racism. Building on this, we examine what contribution the emerging field of educational racism research can make to current research on teacher education. By examining the relationship between the two fields of research, we aim to contribute to the differentiation of teacher education research in a  society of migration.

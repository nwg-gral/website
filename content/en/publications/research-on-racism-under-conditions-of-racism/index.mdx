This article deals with questions of research ethics in the field of educational and social science research on racism. It focuses on key aspects of engaging with research participants in the context of racism research, as well as relevant needs of, and support options for, researchers in the field of racism research. Based on experiences from the ongoing research project “Conditions for successful racism-sensitive teacher education (GraL)”, considerations are made on elements of systematic research reflection as part of the approach to (more) ethically appropriate research practice. &#x20;
